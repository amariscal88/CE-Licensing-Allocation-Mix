ℹ️ Dashboard Intent & Summary

What it does: Geographic regulatory compliance and capacity matching.

Core Focus: This dashboard answers the question: “Are our clinicians licensed in the right places to handle state-specific demand spikes?”

Key Features:
  • Maps out a matrix of Clinical Evaluators against the 28+ state territories they are legally allowed to practice in.
  • Allows you to switch a CE's availability "ON" or "OFF" for specific states, automatically re-weighting their calendar capacity to cover localized backlogs.
  • Provides a rolling strategy graph comparing state-by-state demand against actual state coverage limits.
